Free access

In Today, you can browse today, the previous five days, and the next five days for free. This range moves with the local date on your device. In the Yearbook, you can preview the current month and open details for its first two days.

Free Home Screen widgets use a fixed illustration, while the date and quote update with the day’s content. The Wallpaper Workshop lets you browse five locked previews: the initial position and two on either side. Changing layouts, viewing wallpapers in full, and exporting in HD require Full Access.

Full Access features

Browse all illustrations and quotes: remove Today’s free date limit, switch years and months in the Yearbook, and open details for all Yearbook cards.

Daily artwork updates for Home Screen widgets: when Change Artwork Daily is enabled, widgets use the illustration associated with the day’s calendar content. Actual widget refresh timing is scheduled by the system.

Customize Home Screen widget display: choose whether to show the date and quote. In Chinese mode, you can also choose whether to show the lunar date.

Browse all wallpapers: view all wallpapers in the Wallpaper Workshop.

Choose your wallpaper layout: switch between Original Layout and Artwork Only.

Export and share all wallpapers in HD: export wallpapers and use the system share sheet to save or share them.
