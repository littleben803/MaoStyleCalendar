Scope

This app is provided by Hangzhou IdeaBox Technology Co., Ltd. (“we” or “us”). This policy explains how we handle information when you use the app.

Effective date: November 14, 2026

Local data

You do not need to create an app account. Display preferences and other app settings are stored on your device. The app and widgets share display settings and necessary unlock status through an on-device App Group to display widget content and apply verified purchase entitlements. These local settings are not uploaded to a developer server.

In-app purchases

Full Access is available through Apple In-App Purchase. Apple processes payments. The app uses StoreKit to obtain and verify purchase and entitlement status so it can unlock features, restore purchases, and reflect refunds or revocations. Necessary unlock status is stored on your device and shared with widgets on that device.

We do not receive your Apple Account password or payment card details, and we do not upload transaction information read by the app to a developer server. Apple’s handling of account, payment, and transaction information is governed by its privacy policy.

Permissions and wallpaper export

The app does not read your photo library or request access to contacts, location, the camera, or the microphone. Wallpaper export starts at your request. The app generates a file on your device and uses the system share sheet to send it to the destination you select.

If you choose Save Image, the system may request permission to add the wallpaper to Photos. If you select another app or service, its handling of the file is governed by that provider’s practices.

External services

Opening Apple’s Standard EULA connects to Apple’s website. Purchasing and restoring purchases may also require a connection to Apple services. Apple’s handling of related information is governed by its privacy policy.

Managing data

You can change display preferences in the app. The app does not provide cloud syncing for app preferences. System backups and restores depend on your device settings and system rules.

When you delete the app, removal of local app data and shared containers is managed by the system. Wallpapers saved to Photos or other locations are not automatically deleted with the app; manage them in those locations. Deleting the app does not delete your Apple purchase records. After reinstalling, you can use Restore Purchases with the original purchasing Apple Account to recover valid entitlements.

Updates and contact

We will update this policy when our data practices change and explain relevant changes as required. For questions about this policy or data handling, contact us through Developer Support on the app’s App Store page.

Usage analytics (iOS app)

The iOS app uses Firebase Analytics, provided by Google, to understand feature usage and improve the product. Analytics is enabled by default. Firebase may process app identifiers, device information, feature interactions, purchase-related data, and approximate location.

We do not send reading content, wallpaper images, photos, or payment card details to Firebase. We do not use advertising identifiers or use analytics data for personalized advertising.

Google may process analytics data outside your country or region. See Google’s Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and Firebase privacy information (https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy). For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact privacy@ideaboxapps.com.
